# Changelog

Every release of limn, newest first. Versions before 1.0 may change the API
in any release.

## Unreleased

- **Breaking**: the minimum supported Rust version is 1.99, up from 1.95. CI and the site build use Rust 1.99.1
- `limn_egui`: a picture or fill rounded enough of its own to lie inside a rounded clip's arc keeps its own corners, as a 40 thumbnail rounded by 8 near the end of a 56 pill does; it took the clip's concentric corner (20 there), which no longer met its own rim and left the rim standing out

See [Backend](docs/backend.md#draw-commands)
- `Markdown` draws pipe tables: a header row over a delimiter row (`---`, `:--`, `--:`, `:-:` line its column up), rows to the next line without `|`, `\|` for a pipe in a cell. The header is bold over the panel's fill, columns take the width their cells ask and share it when they do not fit, wrapping inside; a table wider than that scrolls sideways. See [Markdown](docs/components/markdown.md#tables)
- A single-line field submits on a line break typed alone, as a soft keyboard's Return comes on iOS, with no Enter key; a text area still takes it as a break. A physical Enter is unchanged: limn-egui already pairs its key and its text into one Enter
- `UiTree::set_spatial_focus(true)`: the arrow keys move the focus to the nearest control in their direction, as a television's remote does, and bring it into view, even from out of sight in a scroll area, as a shelf of cards under the view that scrolls sideways; within an open popover, among its own. The focused control keeps the arrows it uses (a slider, a tab strip, a menu, a field's caret), and Up and Down leave a single-line field. Controls in the focused one's lane come first; out of it Up and Down reach the nearest, Left and Right only what stands less than its height off, so the end of a row does not drop into the next. With nothing focused, an arrow focuses the first control. A slider keeps the arrows it moves by until it is at that end, where the arrow moves the focus on, so the focus can always leave it. Off by default. See [Accessibility](docs/accessibility.md#what-you-have-to-do)
